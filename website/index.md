---
layout: home

hero:
  name: Kurobayomi
  text: Anime and manga on the Linux desktop
  tagline: A GTK 4 port of Aniyomi. Watch anime and read manga from the sources you choose, in one native app.

features:
  - title: Sources you add
    details: Kurobayomi ships with no sources. Paste the link of a repository you trust and its anime and manga sources appear in Browse.
  - title: Library and updates
    details: Keep the titles you follow in your Library. Updates lists new episodes and chapters; History shows where you stopped.
  - title: Manga reader
    details: Turn pages with the arrow keys, the space bar or the mouse wheel. The last page of a chapter leads into the next one.
  - title: Anime player
    details: Quality, subtitles, audio and speed menus, a video-only full screen, and a Skip button for openings and endings.
  - title: Downloads
    details: Download episodes and chapters to watch and read offline. They are saved in a Kurobayomi folder in your home folder.
  - title: Aniyomi backups
    details: Bring your library over from Aniyomi or Tachiyomi with their .tachibk backup files.

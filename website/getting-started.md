# Getting started

Kurobayomi lets you watch anime and read manga on your computer. It comes with no sources of its own: you add a
**repository**, a link that someone you trust gives you, and its sources show up in the app. This takes about five
minutes. If you do not have the app yet, see [Download](/download).

## 1. Open the app

The first time you open Kurobayomi, you see the welcome screen. There is nothing to set up first.

![The welcome screen, with the Add a source repository button](/screenshots/01-welcome.png)

## 2. Add a repository

A repository is a list of sources that someone publishes at a web address. Kurobayomi does not choose one for you.

1. Click **Add a source repository**.
2. Paste the repository link you were given.

   ![The Add a repository window](/screenshots/02-add-repository.png)

3. Click **Add**. Kurobayomi downloads the repository's sources; this takes a few seconds.

   ![A repository link pasted into the window](/screenshots/03-paste-link.png)

::: tip
If a website shows an **Add to Aniyomi** or **Add to Tachiyomi** button for a repository, clicking it opens Kurobayomi
with the link already filled in.
:::

## 3. Your sources

When the repository is added, Kurobayomi takes you to **Browse**. The **Anime** and **Manga** buttons at the top switch
between the two kinds of sources.

![Anime sources in Browse](/screenshots/05-sources-anime.png)

![Manga sources in Browse](/screenshots/06-sources-manga.png)

Click a source to see what it has. The **Popular** list opens first.

![A source's titles as a grid of covers](/screenshots/07-source-titles.png)

## 4. Search

Click the search button 🔍 next to **Kurobayomi** at the top of the sidebar (in a small window it is at the left of the
top bar), type and press **Enter**. Kurobayomi looks through all your sources of the kind you are viewing and shows
the results per source.

![Search results from all sources](/screenshots/08-search.png)

## 5. Watch or read

Click a title to open its page: the cover, the details and the list of episodes or chapters.

![A title's page with its episode list and the Start button](/screenshots/09-details.png)

- **Start** begins, **Resume** goes on where you stopped.
- **Add to library** keeps the title in your **Library**.

**Anime** plays inside the app. The player has the time bar, volume, the next and previous episode, menus for quality
and server, for subtitles and audio, and for speed. Full screen shows only the video; **Esc** leaves it. When an
opening or an ending plays, a **Skip** button appears.

**Manga** opens in the reader. Turn pages with the arrow keys, the space bar or the mouse wheel. The last page of a
chapter leads straight into the next one.

Kurobayomi remembers where you stopped in every title.

## 6. Your library

Titles you add show up in **Library**. Until you add one, it tells you how to find something.

![The empty Library with a Browse button](/screenshots/10-library-empty.png)

The sidebar also has:

- **Updates**: new episodes and chapters of the titles in your library.
- **History**: what you watched and read recently.
- **Downloads**: what is downloading and what is waiting.

At the bottom of the sidebar are **Incognito** (while it is on, nothing goes into History), **Keyboard shortcuts**,
**Help**, **About** and **Settings**. The **⋮** menu next to the search button holds what the current page can do, for
example adding a folder of your own files to the Library. In a small window the sidebar becomes a bar at the bottom,
and the rest is in its **☰** menu.

## Where your files go

Downloads and backups go to **Kurobayomi** in your home folder: **Anime** and **Manga** hold the downloads, **Backups**
the backups and **Local** your own folders. To use another folder, open **Settings → Data and storage** and click
**Storage location**. New downloads go there after a restart; files already downloaded stay where they are.

## Restore a backup

You can bring back a backup from Kurobayomi, Aniyomi or Tachiyomi (a file that ends in `.tachibk`): click **Restore
from a backup** on the welcome screen, or open **Settings → Data and storage** and click **Restore backup**.

## Connect AniList (optional)

To keep your progress on AniList, open **Settings → Sources and tracking** and log in to AniList.

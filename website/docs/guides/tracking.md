# Tracking

A tracker keeps a list of what you watch and read on a website. Kurobayomi can send your progress to **AniList** and
**MyAnimeList** as you go.

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings → Sources and tracking** and find **Trackers**.
2. Click **AniList** or **MyAnimeList**. Your browser opens the tracker's login page.
3. Log in and allow Kurobayomi.

- **AniList** sends you back to Kurobayomi through a `kurobayomi://` link. Keep Kurobayomi open while you log in.
  Only the stable app takes these links; sign in to AniList with it.
- **MyAnimeList** sends you back to a page on your own computer that hands the login to Kurobayomi. If that page does
  not load, paste the link it shows into the box Kurobayomi offers.

## Track a title

Open a title in your library and use its tracking button to pick the matching entry on the tracker. From then on:

- your progress is sent when you finish an episode or chapter, while **Update progress after reading/watching** is on,
- the Library can show only tracked titles and sort by tracker score (**View options**).

Nothing is sent while [Incognito](/docs/faq/general#what-does-incognito-do) is on.

## Sign out

Click the tracker again in **Settings → Sources and tracking → Trackers** and choose to log out.

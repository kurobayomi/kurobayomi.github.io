# Categories

Categories split your library into tabs, for example "Watching", "Plan to watch" and "Finished". Anime and manga have
their own categories.

## Create, rename and delete

1. Open **Settings → Library**.
2. Click **Edit anime categories** or **Edit manga categories**.
3. Then:
   - **Add category** makes a new one.
   - The pencil renames one (**Rename category**).
   - The switch hides one from the Library.
   - **Delete category** removes one. Its titles stay in your library.
   - Drag a category to change the order of the tabs.

## Put titles in a category

- **When you add a title**, Kurobayomi asks which categories it goes in.
- **Later**, select titles in the Library and choose **Set categories**.

To skip the question, set **Default anime category** or **Default manga category** in **Settings → Library**. It is
**Always ask** at first.

## Move between tabs

Click a tab, or press **Ctrl+Page Up** and **Ctrl+Page Down**. **F5** updates only the category you are viewing.

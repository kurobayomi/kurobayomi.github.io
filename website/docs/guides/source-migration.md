# Source migration

Migration moves a title in your library to another source and keeps your progress. Use it when a source stops working
or another one has better quality.

## Migrate a title

1. Open the title from your **Library**.
2. Open its **⋮** menu and click **Migrate**, or press **Ctrl+M**.
3. Kurobayomi searches your other sources. Pick the matching title.
4. In **Select data to include**, choose what moves:
   - **Chapters** or **Episodes**: which ones you read or watched
   - **Categories**
   - **Custom cover** and **Custom background**
   - **Delete downloaded**: removes the old title's downloads
5. Click:
   - **Migrate** to replace the old title with the new one, or
   - **Copy** to add the new one and keep the old one too.

Tracking always moves with the title.

## Keyboard

In the dialog, **Ctrl+Enter** migrates and **Ctrl+Shift+Enter** copies.

## Things to know

- Progress moves by chapter or episode number. If the new source numbers them differently, check your progress after.
- A title split into seasons cannot be migrated to a title with plain episodes, or the other way around.

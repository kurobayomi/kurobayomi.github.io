# Reader

## Which reading modes are there?

- **Paged, left to right**: most comics.
- **Paged, right to left**: most manga.
- **Paged, vertical**: one page under the other, a page at a time.
- **Webtoon**: one long strip with no gaps, for webtoons and manhwa.
- **Continuous vertical**: one long strip with a gap between pages.

Set the mode for all titles with **Default reading mode** in **Settings → Reader**, or for one title with **Reader
settings…** inside the reader.

## How do I turn pages?

The arrow keys, **Page Up** and **Page Down**, the space bar or the mouse wheel. **Home** and **End** jump to the first
and last page. The last page of a chapter leads into the next one. See
[Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/guides/keyboard-shortcuts#reader).

## How do I zoom?

**+** zooms in, **−** zooms out, **0** resets. **Settings → Reader → Paged → Scale type** sets how a page fits the
window (Fit screen, Stretch, Fit width, Fit height, Original size, Smart fit), and **Zoom start position** where a
zoomed page starts.

## Pages have white borders or come in pairs

Turn on **Crop borders** to cut empty borders, and **Split wide pages** to show a double page as two pages. Both are in
**Settings → Reader → Paged**.

## How do I save or share a page?

Right-click it (or press **Shift+F10**) for the page actions: save, copy, **Set as cover** and **Bookmark chapter**.
**Ctrl+S** saves and **Ctrl+C** copies the page you are on.

## Can I change the background?

**Settings → Reader → Display → Background color**: black, gray, white or automatic.

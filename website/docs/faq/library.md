# Library

## How do I add a title to my library?

Open the title and click **Add to library**. If you have categories, Kurobayomi asks which ones, unless you set a
**Default anime category** or **Default manga category** in **Settings → Library**.

## How do I sort or filter my library?

Click **View options** in the Library's top bar. It has four parts:

- **Show only**: Downloaded, Unread or Unwatched, Completed, and Tracked (when you are signed in to a tracker).
- **Sort by**: Alphabetically, Last read or Last seen, Last update, Unread or Unseen count, Latest chapter or episode,
  Date added, and Tracker score (when you are signed in to a tracker).
- **View**: Grid or List.
- **Grid style**: Compact, Comfortable or Cover only.

## How do I put titles in tabs?

With categories. See [Categories](/docs/guides/categories).

## How do I select several titles?

Press and hold a cover, or **Ctrl+click** it. **Shift+click** selects a range, **Ctrl+A** selects all and **Ctrl+I**
inverts the selection. **Esc** clears it.

## Why am I warned about large updates and downloads? {#large-updates}

Sources are websites run by other people. When the app asks one site for many titles at once, the site slows down for
everyone and may block you. Kurobayomi shows a warning when one source has more than 60 titles in a single library
update or download.

To keep updates small:

- Remove titles you no longer follow from your library.
- Update one category at a time: open it and press **F5**.
- Use a longer interval for **Automatic updates**.
- Spread your library over several sources where you can.

## Why did a title not update?

To spare the sources, a library update skips titles whose source is turned off, and titles that:

- are completed,
- you have not started,
- still have unread chapters or unwatched episodes,
- are not expected to have a new one yet.

If a title failed, the notice "N update(s) failed" opens the error log. See
[Library update errors](/docs/guides/troubleshooting#library-update-errors).

# Browse and sources

## What is a repository?

A **repository** is a list of sources published at a web address. You add it in **Settings → Sources and tracking →
Repositories**, or click **Add a source repository** on the welcome screen. A website's **Add to Kurobayomi** button
opens the app with its link filled in.

## Why is Browse empty after adding a repository?

Every source of a new repository starts **off**. Open the repository under **Settings → Sources and tracking →
Repositories** and turn on the sources you want. A source that is off is not downloaded, not shown in Browse or search,
and skipped by library updates.

## How do I remove a repository?

Open it in **Settings → Sources and tracking → Repositories** and click **Delete repo**. Titles from its sources stay in
your library.

## A title says "This source is off"

Its source is turned off. Click **Turn on** to use it again.

## What do the source statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Working** | The last check reached the site. |
| **Temporarily blocked by the site** | The site refused the app for now. Try again later. |
| **Broken: waiting for a fix** | The site changed and the source needs an update from its repository. |

Click **Check now** to check a source again.

## Kurobayomi asks me to trust an extension

An extension Kurobayomi does not know shows **Untrusted extension**: a harmful one could read your stored logins. Click
**Trust** only if you know where it comes from, otherwise **Uninstall**. **Revoke trusted unknown extensions** in **Settings → Sources and tracking →
Extensions** takes your trust back.

## How do I search every source at once?

Use the search button 🔍 next to **Kurobayomi** at the top of the side panel. It searches all sources that are turned on,
for the kind you are viewing (anime or manga), and lists the results per source.

## How do I see adult titles?

Turn on **Show adult (18+) titles** in **Settings → Sources and tracking**.

## A source shows a Cloudflare check

See [Cloudflare checks](/docs/guides/troubleshooting#cloudflare-checks).

## Who do I report a broken source to?

To the people who publish the repository, not to Kurobayomi. Kurobayomi does not make or host sources.

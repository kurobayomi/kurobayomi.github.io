# Updates

## How do I check for new episodes and chapters?

Click **Update library** in **Library** or **Updates**, or press **F5** in the Library to update the category you are
viewing. New episodes and chapters show in **Updates**.

## Can Kurobayomi check by itself?

Yes. Open **Settings → Library → Global update** and choose **Automatic updates**: every 12 hours, daily, every 2 days,
every 3 days or weekly. The first automatic update runs as soon as it can after you turn it on.

::: info
Kurobayomi has no background service. Automatic updates, backups and downloads only run while the app is open.
:::

## Can new episodes download by themselves?

Yes. In **Settings → Downloads and storage → Auto-download**, turn on **Download new episodes** or **Download new
chapters**.

## What is Upcoming? {#upcoming}

**Upcoming**, in the menu of the **Updates** page, is a calendar of when titles in your library are expected to get
their next episode or chapter. Kurobayomi guesses the date from when earlier ones came out.

### Why is a title missing from Upcoming?

A title shows in Upcoming only when all of this is true:

- It is in your **Library**.
- Its status is **Ongoing** or **Publishing finished** (for anime also **Upcoming**). Completed, cancelled, on-hiatus
  and unknown titles are left out.
- Kurobayomi has a predicted date for it. A title needs a few updates before a date can be guessed.
- The predicted date is today or later.

The date is a guess, not a schedule from the source.

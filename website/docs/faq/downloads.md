# Downloads

## Where are my downloads?

In the **Kurobayomi** folder in your home folder (your user folder on Windows), unless you chose another **Storage
location**: **Anime** and **Manga** hold the downloads, sorted by source, then title. The nightly app uses
**Kurobayomi-Nightly**.

## In which format are they saved?

Chapters are saved as `.cbz` files, episodes as `.mkv` files. Both open in other apps too.

## Which quality does an episode download in?

The **Default quality** of **Settings → Player**. When no server of the episode has that quality, the best one is
downloaded.

## Do I need ffmpeg?

No. ffmpeg comes with Kurobayomi on Linux and Windows.

## How many download at once?

Set **Maximum downloads** in **Settings → Downloads and storage**.

## Can downloads be deleted after I watch or read them?

Yes. **Settings → Downloads and storage → Delete chapters/episodes → After reading/watching automatically delete**
deletes the last one you finished, or one up to five before it.

## What happens if I close the app while downloading?

Kurobayomi asks before quitting. The queue continues the next time you open it.

## A download fails with too little space

Kurobayomi needs at least 200 MB free on the drive of your storage location. Free some space or choose another
**Storage location**.
